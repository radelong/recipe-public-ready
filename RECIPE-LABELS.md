# Recipe types and sublabels

Use one main type for where a dish belongs in the cookbook and several sublabels to describe it. For example: Entrée + Chicken + Curry + Indian + One pot; Dessert + Cookies + Chocolate + Baked + Make ahead. Choose the main type by how you serve the recipe. Brunch foods can use Breakfast with Brunch as a label. Bread served as a side still belongs under Bread.

## Label guidance

Keep cuisine, ingredients, and cooking method as separate labels, following the distinctions in [Schema.org Recipe](https://schema.org/Recipe). Dish styles draw inspiration from [King Arthur’s recipe index](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes) and [Allrecipes’ categories](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipes/). This is an expandable starting catalog, not a claim that every possible recipe label is listed.

Dietary labels are manually assigned descriptions. They are not automatically verified allergen or nutritional guarantees. Check the full ingredients and preparation before serving. Planning labels such as 30 minutes or less and Freezer-friendly are also assigned by the author.
